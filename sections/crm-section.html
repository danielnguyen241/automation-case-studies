Agency Operations CRM (WordPress, self-built)

A self-built operations portal that puts a WordPress front-end on the team’s Google Sheets. Staff keep working in Sheets; the CRM syncs on a schedule or with one click and turns the data into staff, schedule, task-log, client-project, SOP and appraisal modules, so leadership sees who did what, for which client, in one place.

WorkflowGoogle Sheets → Sync → CRM modules → Reports
  • 1,052 task logs synced · 94% avg deep-work score
  • 7 modules: staff, schedule, tasks, projects, SOPs, reviews, skills matrix
  • 33 linked sheets & Looker Studio / GSC reports
  • 0 data re-entry: Sheets stay the source of truth, CSV export built in
